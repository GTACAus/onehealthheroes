pH Measuring Kit

The pH level of something tells us how acidic or basic it is. The pH scale goes from 0 to 14.

Measuring the pH of a water or soil sample is important because it tells us about water and soil quality in an area. It’s helpful to know how acidic or basic something is as different plants and animals can’t survive in environments that have a pH level that is too high or too low.

The pH Measuring kit is used to measure the pH of a substance. It tells you how acidic or basic a water or soil sample is.

The STEM Cycle of Inquiry: Predict Stage

A prediction is what you think will happen in an experiment, before you do it.

Use the following template to write your guess.

"If I ____, then I think ____ will happen because ____."

If I...What will you change or test?
Then I think...What do you think will happen?
Because...Why do you think that? Use your own ideas or what you already know.

Example: If I test the pH of the soil in the planter box, then I think it will be neutral because plants grow best in soil that isn't too acidic or too alkaline.

Write your prediction on page 6 of your logbook

Safety Considerations

When using equipment, we need to always be safe

Read and follow the below safety instructions when using this kit

Storage and Cleaning

Wash the water pH meter with distilled water after each use so it stays accurate.

Safe Handling and Storage

The water pH meter has glass parts and can break. Notify an adult of any spills or broken glass.

Store chemicals in a safe place away from reach of children and pets

Protect your eyes

Wear safety goggles when mixing chemicals with dirt.
