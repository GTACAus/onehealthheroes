Boson Kit

The Boson Kit is a box of smart science tools that you can plug together to explore the world around you. Inside the kit is a micro:bit (a tiny computer that can collect information and show results on its screen).

This kit also includes lots of easy-to-use sensors, like ones that can measure humidity, light, temperature, or pH. When you connect the sensors to the micro:bit, you can design and run your own investigations.

06/11

STEM Cycle of Inquiry: Predict Stage

A prediction is what you think will happen in an experiment, before you do it.
Use the following template to write your guess.

"If I ____, then I think ____ will happen because ____."

If I...What will you change or test?
Then I think...What do you think will happen?
Because...Why do you think that? Use your own ideas or what you already know.

Example:
If I grow spinach seeds, then I think I won’t find it will grow best in soil temperatures between 10°C to 21°C because this is the optimal temperature range for spinach.

Write your prediction (Q2 on page 4 of your logbook)

07/11

STEM Cycle of Inquiry: Plan Stage

Once you have asked a question and made an educated guess, you need to plan your investigation. This means deciding what you are going to do to test your guess.

Consider the following questions to plan your investigation:

  • What will you do?
  • What equipment will you use?

Example:
We will use Boson Kits to observe and record what soil temperature is best for growing (your seed type).

Fill in the blank with your seed type (Q3, pg 5 of your logbook)

08/11

Safety Considerations

When using equipment, we always need to be safe

Burn risk

Turn off power supply when not in use.

The circuit can get warm - do not touch the circuit when it is on

Electrical Components

Avoid getting water/liquid on the circuit

If you do, turn off the power supply and dry the circuit completly before using it again

Sharp edges

Hold pieces gently and do not force pieces into the circuit.

This protects your hands and the equipment.

Record 1-2 safety considerations on page 5 of your logbook
